Benevolence

Once when I was young
I had many balloons
One was red, one was blue, one was yellow
I always took them with me to the park

They were all different sizes
but
I loved each the same

The yellow one grew so big and wide
And flew up so high
That it turned into a new Sun

And the red one turned into a new Mars

And the blue one turned into a new Neptune

When the calendar made me 13
I looked down and realized I was no longer holding the balloon strings

Every now and then, the Sun seems a bit closer than usual
